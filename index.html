The Market Landscape

A live, slice-able view of the Korean pharma market — class spend, manufacturer concentration, disease mix, and payer split, all queryable by therapeutic area.

Illustrative · Full Data Wired
How this tool works
Method · Data · Gaps
Methodology 4 steps
  1. Filter HIRA all-payer utilization records by ATC class within the selected TA, scoped to diag_ym window.
  2. Aggregate msup_use_amt and tot_use_qty by month, class, manufacturer, and ICD-10 indication.
  3. Compute originator/generic shares from is_originator, class CAGR from monthly time series, payer mix from insup_tp_cd.
  4. Render time series, class rankings, manufacturer concentration, disease mix, and payer split.

Pack 01 · Market Overview · Tool 02

Whitespace Mapping

Where in Korean pharma is the unmet opportunity? Diseases with high treated burden but few therapy options, surfaced as quantified whitespace.

Illustrative · Full Data Wired
How this tool works
Method · Data · Gaps
Methodology 4 steps
  1. Aggregate treated patient volume per ICD-10 code (sick_cd) from HIRA utilization data — proxy for disease burden.
  2. Count reimbursed therapy options per indication using ATC class plus reimbursement status; surface manufacturer concentration.
  3. Compute gap score as a function of treated burden, treatment density, and class growth — weighted to highlight under-treated diseases.
  4. Rank diseases by gap score; render bubble matrix and ranked opportunity list. Whitespace = high burden × low treatment density.

v3 · True Unmet Need

Patent expiration — needed to flag classes where patents expired but few generics launched (regulatory/commercial barrier or opportunity).

Pack 02 · Launch Pack · Tool 01

The Launch Analogue

Cohort of US→KR launch precedents, weighted by similarity across class, indication, modality, mechanism, orphan status, and US-tier. Load-bearing for the rest of the Launch Pack.

Illustrative · 4 of 6 dims wired
Defensible w/ disclosure
How this tool works
Method · Data · Gaps
Methodology 5 steps
  1. Anchor the subject drug to its US originator via Drugs@FDA application_number and KR trust_item_seq (backend join, never displayed).
  2. Build candidate universe: all KR drugs with a US-originator anchor that completed MFDS filing 2014–present.
  3. Score each candidate across 6 similarity dimensions — class, indication, modality, mechanism, orphan status, US-tier — with prior weights.
  4. Apply dimension confidence multipliers (wired dims = 1.0; unwired dims = 0.0 contribution + flagged).
  5. Rank by weighted similarity; surface top 14 with full dimension breakdown. Cohort feeds Timing & Price forecasts downstream.
Data Inputs · Wired 12 fields
item_seq item_eng_name main_ingr_eng atc_code is_originator is_orphan_drug mix_type form_name route_of_administration narcotic_kind_code trial_phase clnc_test_sn trust_item_seq (backend) Drugs@FDA (backend)

Methodology weights mechanism at 0.20 — largest single hole. Needs ATC-5 + DrugBank/RxNorm hierarchy join. Cohort runs with 4 wired dims; mechanism dim greyed out.
v1 · Modality Classifier

Small-mol / biologic / cell-gene derivation rule. material_name wired, induty_type not. Need to author + validate classifier against manual sample.

The Product Market

The class-specific competitive landscape where the molecule will enter — narrower than Market Landscape, scoped to the analogue cohort's class universe.

Illustrative · KR-clean
Defensible w/ disclosure
How this tool works
Method · Data · Gaps
Methodology 5 steps
  1. Take subject drug + its analogue cohort from Launch Analogue; derive the relevant class universe (ATC-4 + adjacent ATC-5 codes).
  2. Pull all HIRA-utilized drugs in the class universe, scoped to the indication's ICD-10 set (sick_cd).
  3. Aggregate msup_use_amt by month and originator/generic status; compute class CAGR and concentration.
  4. Position the subject drug's projected price band against current incumbents using HIRA mx_cprc distribution (when wired).
  5. Render class trajectory, top incumbents, indication mix, payer mix, and price-band positioning — all class-scoped.

msup_use_amt tot_use_qty entp_eng_name atc_code is_originator sick_nm sick_cd diag_ym insup_tp_cd
KR-only scope resolves the wire's cross-market 🔴 flags on diag_ym granularity and insup_tp_cd scope. Korea HIRA all-payer monthly is the entire view.
